Anunnaki - 1

അനുനാകി - 1 · അടിമകളുടെ ഉടയോൻ

Before the stars were named, before the first towers scraped the heavens, they walked among us as rulers of wind and stone. We sang their hymns in silent obedience, tilling soil that was never ours to keep.

"Remember who shaped the clay of your spirit," the elder whispered, his hand resting upon the carved cuneiform monolith. "They did not give us life out of mercy; they gave us life so we might carry their burden."

In the shadows of the ancient zigurat, the dawn began to break—a crimson horizon signaling the dawn of a rebellion that would echo across ages.
