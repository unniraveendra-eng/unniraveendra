Biography

Unniraveendra

Author · Novelist · Creator of the Anunnaki Universe

Unniraveendra is a Kerala-based author whose writing weaves together ancient mythology, cosmic mystery, and deep human emotion. With a deep fascination for historical lore and speculative fantasy, his work invites readers to question humanity's place in the cosmos.

His debut flagship novel, Anunnaki - 1: Adimagalude Udayon (അനുനാകി - 1 · അടിമകളുടെ ഉടയോൻ), marks the opening chapter of an expansive fiction universe that bridges epic historical themes with sci-fi mystery.

"Literature allows us to traverse centuries in a single breath. In writing Anunnaki, my aim was to craft a mythology that feels both anciently familiar and astonishingly new."
